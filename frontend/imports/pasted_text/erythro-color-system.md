# ERYTHRONET — LOCKED VISUAL COLOR SYSTEM

## NON-NEGOTIABLE BRAND DIRECTION

The primary visual identity of **ErythroNet MUST be RED + WHITE**.

This is a medical/emergency blood coordination platform, so the interface must immediately communicate:

**BLOOD + HEALTHCARE + EMERGENCY + TRUST + MODERN TECHNOLOGY**

The design should look like a modern technology product while retaining a clear medical identity.

### PRIMARY VISUAL FORMULA

**70% White / Light Surfaces**
**20% Red / Crimson**
**10% Neutral Dark Text + Supporting Status Colors**

Red and white must dominate the overall product experience.

Do NOT reinterpret this product as a dark-mode-first website.

Do NOT introduce purple, blue, cyan, violet, or neon gradients as primary design colors.

# FINAL COLOR QUALITY CHECK

Before completing the design, verify:

1. Does the product immediately feel related to blood and healthcare?
2. Is red clearly the primary brand color?
3. Is white the dominant UI surface?
4. Are internal pages mostly white/light?
5. Is crimson used strategically rather than everywhere?
6. Are amber and green restricted to functional statuses?
7. Are purple/blue/cyan completely absent from the brand system?
8. Does the interface still feel technologically modern?
9. Does the glassmorphism look medical and premium rather than cyberpunk?
10. Does the neumorphism remain subtle?
11. Does the interface look professional without relying on dark backgrounds?
12. Does the product visually communicate emergency blood coordination within seconds?

If any screen starts looking predominantly black, purple, blue, neon or cyberpunk:

**REDESIGN IT.**

The final visual identity must remain:

## RED + WHITE
